About this show

Write a few sentences about your show here: the song, the idea, and what to watch for.

It is a light, colour and texture show for a 3D model, set to music and timed to the beat. It was choreographed in MapHappy (my projection-mapping tool) and is played here by a renderer written in plain JavaScript and WebGL 2, with no graphics libraries. Every surface, texture generator and light is drawn by my own shaders: Phong, Blinn-Phong and Toon lighting with ambient, directional, point and spot lights, each light model its own fragment shader program, swapped with useProgram.
