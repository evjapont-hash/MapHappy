Making a show

  1. Add your song: Media tab → Add files…. It goes on the timeline and its tempo is detected, so the beats show and keyframes snap to them.
  2. Add surfaces (Surfaces tab): drag their corners on the stage to warp them onto your object. Pick their Picture: a generator, a video, or the test pattern.
  3. 3D model: + 3D Object, then Load OBJ… (or a built-in cube/sphere/cylinder). Turn Lighting up and set the Sun, Point light and Spotlight; pick Phong, Blinn-Phong or Toon.
  4. Animate: move the playhead, set a value, press ◆. Move the playhead again, change the value: once a setting has a keyframe, changing it adds one at the playhead.
  5. Presets (Presets tab): store looks on the keys 1–9 and 0, and play them live with Live on.
  6. Publish: Download for the web → unzip into your repository's web/ folder (replacing web/show) → commit and push. The published page then plays it.

Your work is autosaved in this browser (files you add too). Save downloads the project file, which also opens in the desktop MapHappy.

  • Space play / pause · ← → step (Shift: 1 s) · Home start · M marker
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S save · Delete selected keyframe or song clip · B blackout
  • Song clips: drag to move, drag an end to trim, S splits at the playhead.
  • Freeform: double-click an edge to add a point, right-click a point to delete it.
