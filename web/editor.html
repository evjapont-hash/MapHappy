Making a show

  1. Add your song: Media tab → Add files…. It goes on the timeline and its tempo is detected, so the beats show and keyframes snap to them.
  2. Add surfaces (Surfaces tab): drag their corners on the stage to warp them onto your object. Pick their Picture: a generator, a video, or the test pattern.
  3. 3D model: 3D Object, then Load OBJ… (or a built-in cube/sphere/cylinder). Turn Lighting up and set the Sun, Point light and Spotlight; pick Phong, Blinn-Phong or Toon.
  4. Animate: move the playhead, set a value, press the keyframe button () next to it. Move the playhead again, change the value: once a setting has a keyframe, changing it adds one at the playhead.
  5. Presets (Presets tab): store looks on the keys 1–9 and 0, and play them live with Live on.
  6. Projector: Output opens a window with only the stage. Drag it onto the projector, double-click it for full screen, and line your surfaces up on the real object while you watch it.
  7. Publish: Download for the web → unzip into your repository's web/ folder (replacing web/show) → commit and push. The published page then plays it.

Your work is autosaved in this browser (files you add too). Save downloads the project file, which also opens in the desktop MapHappy.

  • Space play / pause · ← → step (Shift: 1 s) · Home start · M marker
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S save · B blackout
  • Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy / paste the selected surface · Delete or Backspace deletes what is selected (surface, mask, keyframe or song clip)
  • Stage: scroll to zoom in, drag an empty part (or with the middle mouse button) to move around, + / - zoom, click the % to fit it again.
  • Keyframes: Shift- or Ctrl-click to select several, or Shift-drag a box round them; Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy them and paste them at the playhead (Ctrl+Shift+V: at the same time as the copy). To paste onto another surface, select it (or click one of its rows) first. Delete deletes them.
  • Clips: drag a clip's header to move it, its right edge to stretch it (its keyframes then loop) and its left edge to move its start. Click a clip's header, then Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copies it, onto another surface too. Right-click a clip to duplicate it, make a ghost copy (it shares the keyframes: change one, both change) or delete it; right-click an empty spot to make a new clip.
  • Timeline: drag a keyframe sideways to change its time and up or down to change its value (Shift: only the value). Drag a row's bottom edge to make it taller or shorter; drag the scroll bars, or scroll over the track names to scroll up and down.
  • Drag the borders between the panels to resize them; double-click a border to reset it.
  • Surfaces list: Ctrl-click or Shift-click to select several, then drag them onto a folder together.
  • Hold Shift while dragging a corner to resize the whole shape without distorting it. Click an empty part of the stage to deselect.
  • Song clips: drag to move, drag an end to trim, S splits at the playhead.
  • Freeform: double-click an edge to add a point, right-click a point to delete it.
